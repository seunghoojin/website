공간과 로봇의 공존을 위한 설계

Notes on corridors, thresholds and signage

2 November 2026

로봇과 사람이 같은 복도를 쓰려면 폭, 회전 반경, 시야가 함께 설계되어야 한다. Prior work treats the robot as a vehicle and the building as fixed; this essay reverses the question and asks what a building owes to the machines that move through it [2].

The argument proceeds in three steps. Section 2 sets out the constraints, Section 3 derives rules, and Section 4 tests them on one floor.1

2Constraints

Only indoor circulation is considered: corridors, thresholds, lifts and signage. A shared corridor must let a person with luggage pass a robot without either stopping.

2.1Corridors

A shared corridor is at least 1.8 m wide, with passing bays every 20 m. Narrower corridors force one party to yield, and the robot is usually the one that cannot.
