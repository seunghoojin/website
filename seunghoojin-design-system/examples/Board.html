Research

Space for people and robots

로봇이 일상 공간에 들어오면서 건축의 전제가 바뀌고 있다. I study what corridors, thresholds and lifts owe to the machines that use them, and how a plan changes when it is drawn for both.

Reading plans with models

A multimodal model reads permit drawings and checks them against zoning rules. It is good at counting rooms and poor at reading section cuts.

This work is part of the Department of Architecture and the Interdisciplinary AI program at Seoul National University.
