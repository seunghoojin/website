Definition 1 (Shared corridor)A corridor is shared when people and robots use it in both directions without a separated lane.

Proposition 2A shared corridor narrower than 1.8 m forces one party to yield.

Proof.The robot's envelope is 0.7 m and a person passing with luggage needs 1.1 m, so any narrower width cannot hold both.

Remark 3The bound ignores wheelchairs, which need 1.5 m on their own.
