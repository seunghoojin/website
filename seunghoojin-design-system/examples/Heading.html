3Model architecture

로봇과 사람이 같은 복도를 쓰려면 폭, 회전 반경, 시야가 함께 설계되어야 한다. The rules below follow from that constraint.

3.1Circulation

Corridors.A shared corridor is at least 1.8 m wide, with passing bays every 20 m.

Thresholds.Level changes above 15 mm are ramped, never stepped.

3.1.1Vertical circulation

Elevators are shared, with a call button reachable at robot height.
