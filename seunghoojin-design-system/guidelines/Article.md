An article page: what an entry title opens. The full text is a box two columns wide, with the section's box beside it.

Markup: `<div class="ds-shell ds-shell--article">` (centred at `board-3`, 899px), the Masthead, then `<div class="ds-board">` holding `<article class="ds-article">` (two columns, 597px outside) and a `ds-column` with the section's box, then the Footer. Inside the article: ArticleHeader, optional Abstract and TOC, the body, Footnotes, References, Pagination.

The article box is drawn like every box, with 28px side padding. Its text is 15px Arial on 1.6, about 70 characters a line, with 14px between paragraphs: a step up from the 13px snippets, for long reading. Headings are 18px, 16px and 15px italic, bold. On phones the article takes the full width with 12px padding and 16px text.
