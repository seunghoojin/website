Footnote markers and the notes at the end of an article: a short grey rule, then small grey notes.

Markup: `<sup class="ds-fnref"><a href="#fnN">N</a></sup>` after the punctuation. Notes: `<section class="ds-footnotes">` with an `<ol>`; each `<li>` starts with `<a class="ds-fn__no">N</a>` linking back, then the note, `small` (12px) in `ink-muted`. Notes on the title use symbols (*, †, ‡) in a `ds-footnotes--symbols` section placed first; the two share one rule.
