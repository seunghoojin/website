Numbered headings inside an article, bold like box titles.

Markup: `<h2 class="ds-section">`, `<h3 class="ds-subsection">` and `<h4 class="ds-subsubsection">`, each with an `id`, holding `<a class="ds-num" href="#id">3.1</a>` and the title in a `<span>`. `section` is `box-title` (16px bold `ink`), `subsection` 14px bold, the third level 14px italic. Numbers are optional; leave them out of short articles.

For labelled paragraphs, start the paragraph with `<span class="ds-runin">Encoder:</span>`. Space: 20px above a section, 16px above a subsection, a few pixels before the paragraph.
