An image that runs to the edges of the article box and opens full size on request.

Markup: inside `ds-article`, `<figure class="ds-figure ds-figure--bleed">` reaches through the box's 28px side padding to its outline; the caption stays inside. The image takes `ds-frame` and may be wrapped in `<a class="ds-zoom" href="full-size.jpg">`; load `scripts/ds.js` to open it full size on white, a click or Escape closing it. Use it for the one image a project is about.
