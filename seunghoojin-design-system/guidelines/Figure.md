Figure inside an article: the image in a `ds-frame`, then a small centred caption.

Markup: `<figure class="ds-figure">` with the image given `class="ds-frame"` (see ImageFrame: its own proportions between 2:1 and 4:5, cropped around `--focus` beyond that) and `<figcaption class="ds-figure__caption">` starting with `<span class="ds-figure__label">Figure N:</span>`. The caption is 13px, centred. No frame line or shadow. For panels see Subfigures; for an image that should fill the article box see Plate.
