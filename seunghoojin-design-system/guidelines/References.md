Numbered bibliography at the end of an article, cited in the text as [1].

Markup: `<ol class="ds-refs">` with one `<li>` per source; the bracketed number is generated in `ink-muted` with a hanging indent. `small` (12px) in `ink-body`. Venues in `<span class="ds-ref__venue">` and book titles in `<i>` are italic. Order by first citation.
