The head of an article box: the section name in red, exactly as it heads the box the entry came from (it links back to that section's SectionIndex), then the title, an optional subtitle and the date.

Markup: `<header class="ds-header">` holding `<span class="ds-header__section"><a href="section.html">Writing</a></span>`, `<h1 class="ds-title">` (22px bold), an optional `<p class="ds-subtitle">` (15px), `<p class="ds-byline">` with the date in English ("2 November 2026"), and an optional `<details class="ds-details">` with an underlined "Details" summary. Left-aligned.
