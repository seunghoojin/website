Previous and next links at the foot of an article box, under a 1px `rule`.

Markup: `<ul class="ds-pager">` as the last child of the `ds-article`, one `<li>` per neighbour, each a link holding `ds-pager__dir` ("Previous" or "Next", `ink-muted`) and `ds-pager__title`. 13px; links underlined. Omit a line when there is no neighbour.
