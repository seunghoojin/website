Button for actions such as download, cite, submit. Use a link when the action only navigates.

Markup: `<button class="ds-button">`: a square box with a 1px `rule-strong` border, 13px Arial `ink`. `ds-button--primary` is filled red `accent` with bold `on-accent` text, at most one per view. Disabled: the `disabled` attribute, with a `rule` border and `ink-muted` text. Labels are short verbs in sentence case.
